# Browser client asset provenance and licenses

- Active Expanse artwork: original STRATOKS contributor artwork generated with OpenAI image generation, 2026. `web/public/art/characters.png`, `world.png` and `extra.png` are transparent, locally bundled atlases used by `web/src/expanse/art.tsx`, `ground.ts`, `paths.ts` and `worldRenderer.ts`. Extraction, registration and supplemental art are by the interface contributor. The [runtime manifest](../web/public/art/manifest.json) records dimensions, rectangles, anchors, input hashes, attribution and MIT reuse; it is copied to `dist/art/manifest.json`. See [art integration](art/INTEGRATION.md) and [generation direction](art/DIRECTION.md). Editable raster masters are in `web/art-source/`; no layered painting, skeleton or authored 3D model is claimed. No stock or third-party reference artwork was intentionally incorporated. Generated provenance does not certify uniqueness or copyright eligibility.
- Worn ground uses only a bare-earth 20×12 crop at `(438,449)` within the supplied `world.png` atlas's `arid.ground` sprite. Resource rocks and plant silhouettes are excluded. It is cosmetic paint on confirmed clear land around reported shelters and adjacent positions observed during this viewer session, with no simulation road or resource behavior.
- The earlier code-native renderer in `web/src/renderer.ts`, mascot/icons in `web/src/icons.tsx`, and `web/public/favicon.svg` belong to the archived settlement client. They remain under the client/art MIT license; the production configuration disables that client. Its provenance does not describe the active Expanse atlases.
- Current favicon (`web/public/favicon.png`) derives from the generated character art. Current share image (`web/public/share-world.jpg`, copied into `dist/`) is a rendered recorded-world view. The older `share.png` is a private-demo capture for the archived client. Same client/art license; font and wallet-brand rights remain separate.
- Manrope variable font: Mikhail Sharanda and Mirko Velimirovic, SIL Open Font License 1.1; [license](licenses/manrope.txt). The Latin WOFF2 is bundled locally from the pinned Fontsource dependency. Only weights 400, 600 and 800 are requested.
- MetaMask, Rabby and Phantom wallet marks: copied from RainbowKit's `packages/rainbowkit/src/wallets/walletConnectors/*Wallet/*Wallet.svg`, [upstream](https://github.com/rainbow-me/rainbowkit), [MIT license](licenses/rainbowkit.txt). Coinbase mark: supplied by the assignment's pinned connector reference. Marks identify the corresponding wallets; trademarks remain with their owners. EIP-6963 provider icons come from the installed wallet.
- Better Interface guidance: Jakub Krehel, pinned commit `267330e1adfc66a718fb65fa6918c1f06d0a689e`, MIT. Documentation method: Paul Bakaus, Impeccable, pinned commit `9d715cc4f5564a990ca8345abfdd5df6dc9b41c8`, Apache-2.0. This implementation adapts the methods; both notices/licenses are preserved in [design-guidance.txt](licenses/design-guidance.txt).
- No audio or remote tile service. The world is rendered from authoritative frame data, not represented by a background painting. Dependencies and their exact resolutions are recorded in `web/package-lock.json`; upstream code retains its original licenses.

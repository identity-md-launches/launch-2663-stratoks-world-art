# Phase 2B reviewer revision

This revision preserves the accepted implementation and corrects the reproduced planner, energy, observation, shelter-capacity, follower, death-attribution, and read-only-view defects. The reviewer response file at the repository root records a verdict for every finding.

## Changes

- Planner version 2 evaluates every root affordance and every affordance from each retained beam state. Candidates and equal-score beam entries use canonical ordering, so an affordance array's enumeration order cannot hide rest, explore, home, inspect, social actions, or later combinations. Horizon remains 3 and the beam remains 5.
- Observation version 2 includes locally perceived shelters. Memory version 2 retains up to 16 individually seen or self-built shelter locations. Shelter features, targets, pile choices, local weather, and social affordances come from observation/self/memory; target hunger is not exposed or queried.
- Action and movement energy charges are guarded. Insufficient energy ends the attempt without spending materials or applying damage, and energy remains at or above zero. Stored negative energy is normalized by the v2→v3 migration. Exhaustion is now recorded as a death cause.
- Default inventory capacity is 12, enough for the existing 4 wood + 2 stone shelter recipe. Expanse schema 3 migrates older v1/v2 snapshots, including the old 10-weight limit, while retaining brains, histories, memories and RNG state.
- Followers accumulate the same terrain travel time and weight-sensitive movement cost as walkers. Leadership is read-only; expired follower records are pruned by deterministic simulation ticks. Viewport node display computes regrowth on a copy, and pristine generated chunks are cache-only and omitted from snapshots. The info summary's `chunks` count now reflects dirty persistent chunks, not ephemeral cache entries.

Across paired cold seeds, full had more hand axes than frozen in **10/10** and more fire lighters in **10/10**. Its survivor count was higher in **7/10**, lower in **2/10**, and tied in one. Against no-planner, full made more axes and fire lighters in all 10 seeds, but survivors were higher in only **5/10** and lower in **5/10**; mean exposure deaths were 6.2 full versus 5.1 no-planner. Cold survival is not a reliable benefit of learning/planning: seed 202 ended with 1/1/0 residents and seed 909 with 5/8/12 (full/frozen/no-planner), while seed 404 ended with 31/18/23. These are failures and mixed causal outcomes, not selected success clips.

In the settlement reward-reversal scenario, the mean post/pre berry-eating ratios were 0.806 full, 1.248 frozen, and 1.133 no-planner. This is an observed condition difference, not proof that learning alone caused the change; depletion, toxicity, survival, and planner effects are coupled. Full runs built shelters in 6/10 settlement seeds and 3/10 cold seeds. Cold full runs recorded new-settlement discoveries in 3/10 seeds. Births occurred naturally in the same runs; no success was scripted.

## Remaining limits

- The cold-highlands test still has severe exposure deaths and no consistent survival gain, even when tools and fires are discovered. Unknown deaths remain in some experiment runs, including salt-water damage paths; this revision specifically fixes exhaustion attribution.
- The local HTTP test does not test browser rendering. Docker/Compose execution and ARM64/cgroup resource limits were not tested here; the prior deployment review records these limits. Production remains untouched and owner deployment is still required.

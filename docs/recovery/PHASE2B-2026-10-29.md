## Populated-world migration corrections

Old homes are shelter IDs, relationships are affinity records and pregnancy identifies a partner. Expanse expects coordinates, numeric affinity and father. These are explicitly adapted; original relationship records and an operator-only full legacy state archive are retained. The archive is not exposed by public frames.

World identity, brain matrices, memories, parents and history remain recoverable through snapshots/restarts. Carried wood/stone and shelter stocks become inventory/piles, recorded as imported holdings, without inventing gathered totals. Imported node regrowth begins at migration tick, not zero. Fractional legacy berries are floored for the integer-unit new model; the exact original values remain in the archive. This is a documented rule conversion, not byte-identical ecology.

Due pregnancies are processed before new-conception cooldown/elder-age checks. Pending legacy hazards and unfinished construction are refused rather than silently discarded. A final live-world copy must therefore pass these preflight gates before cutover. No operator should delete a structure or event merely to pass the gates.

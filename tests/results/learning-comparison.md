## Reading these numbers honestly
- Differences are from 8 seeds of a stochastic simulation; compare the paired diff with the sd before concluding anything.
- Visitor-directed behaviour (approach/avoid ratios, distances) is the clearest learned signal: it is driven by the per-wallet opinion that only exists when learning is on.
- Survival/feeding metrics move less: the prior policy is already competent in this ecology, so the bandit mostly fine-tunes it.
